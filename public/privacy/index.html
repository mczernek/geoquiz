Privacy policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

Globik is a free app for learning the world's countries — their flags, capitals and where they are — on a 3D globe, for Android, iOS and the web (globik-app.netlify.app). It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your data is never sold. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, who processes it and what rights you have. It applies to the Android and iOS apps, the web app and these web pages.

In short: your progress is stored on your device and, so it can sync, in our database in the EU under a random anonymous id. An account (email + password, or Google) is optional. From Google we get only your account id, email address, name and profile picture link, use them only to sign you in, and share them with no one. You can delete everything in the app at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is Michał Czernek, an independent developer (a private individual) in Poland, who makes Globik. Contact for everything about your data and this policy: czernekmichal@gmail.com. We haven't appointed a data protection officer (we aren't required to).

2. What data we use and why

Learning progress and settings

For each card (for example "the flag of Poland"): how well you know it, when it's due for review, how many times you answered it right or wrong and when you last saw it; and which topics (flags, capitals, map) and continents you chose. We use it to pick your next questions, show your progress and keep it in sync between your devices. Legal basis: performance of the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Anonymous user id

The first time the app syncs, our authentication provider creates a random user id for your device, so your progress can be saved in the cloud without an account. It isn't linked to your name, your device's advertising id or anything else about you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Account: email address and password (optional)

If you create an account with email and password, we store your email address and your password as a one-way hash (we can't read it). We use your email address to sign you in, to send the emails the sign-in service needs (such as confirming your address) and to answer you. We don't send marketing emails. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Sign in with Google (optional)

If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive basic profile data from Google; see section 3. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — you ask us to sign you in with your Google account.

Technical and security data

When the app talks to our servers, our providers process your IP address and basic request data (time, browser or app version, the page or API requested) to deliver the service. The sign-in service also records sign-in events (time, IP address, browser or app) to protect accounts and stop abuse. These logs are kept for a limited time. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure, working service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

When you write to us

If you email us, we use your email address and what you write to answer you and, for example, to carry out a deletion request. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, where you exercise a right under the GDPR, our legal obligation to handle it (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

What we don't collect

The app doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other device permission. It has no ads, no advertising id, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and no tracking across apps or websites. We don't use your data for profiling or automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects (picking which flag to ask about next isn't one). Your language choice is kept only on your device.

Giving us data is voluntary. The anonymous id and progress sync are needed for your progress to be backed up and synced; without an account everything else still works. An account is optional.

3. Google user data

This section explains how Globik handles data it receives from Google when you sign in with Google.

  • What we access: only the basic sign-in scopes openid, email and profile. Google gives us your Google account id, your email address (and whether Google has verified it), your name and a link to your profile picture. We don't request or get access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data, and we never see your Google password.
  • How we use it: only to sign you in — to create your Globik account, recognize you the next time you sign in, keep your learning progress with your account and show your email address on the Account screen. If you already have a Globik account with the same confirmed email address, Google sign-in opens that account. Your name and profile picture link are saved because Google sends them with the sign-in, but Globik doesn't use or show them.
  • How we store it: with your account in our authentication provider, Supabase, in the European Union (Ireland), encrypted in transit.
  • Sharing: we don't sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. Supabase processes it only on our behalf, to run the sign-in. We don't use it for advertising, don't give it to advertising platforms, data brokers or information resellers, and don't use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. No one reads it, except when you ask us for help with your account, for security reasons (such as investigating abuse) or when the law requires it.
  • Retention and deletion: we keep it as long as your account exists. Account → Delete account in the app deletes it immediately, together with all your progress; you can also ask by email (see how to delete your account). You can remove Globik's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections; this stops sign-in with Google but doesn't delete your Globik account, so delete the account in the app as well if you want your data gone.

Globik's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Where your data is stored

The app works offline, so your progress and settings are always stored on your device: in the app's storage on Android and iOS, and in your browser's local storage on the web. A copy of your progress and settings, your user id and, if you have an account, your email address (and the Google profile data from section 3) are stored in our database in the European Union (Supabase, Ireland), so they can be synced between your devices and restored when you sign in.

5. Who processes your data

We use a few service providers (processors), who process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) — database and sign-in service, hosted in the EU (Ireland). It also sends the sign-in emails.
  • Netlify (Netlify, Inc.) — hosts the web app and these pages (globik-app.netlify.app) and serves them through its worldwide network.

These act as independent controllers, under their own privacy policies:

  • Google — only if you sign in with Google: Google handles your Google account and the sign-in screen.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store — when you download the app. We get from them only the aggregated statistics and crash reports they give every developer.
  • Our email provider (Gmail) — when you write to us.

We don't share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires it (for example, a binding order from a court or authority).

6. Transfers outside the EU

Our database is in the EU. Supabase and Netlify are US companies and Netlify serves the web app from servers around the world, so some data (mainly IP addresses and request data, and access needed for support and maintenance) may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Such transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or on an adequacy decision, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for certified companies. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account, progress, settings, Google profile data: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes them from our database immediately.
  • Anonymous profiles (no account): while you use the app; profiles unused for 60 days or more may be deleted automatically. You can also ask us by email to delete one.
  • Server and sign-in logs: for a short period set by our providers, then deleted automatically.
  • Emails with us: as long as needed to handle your request.
  • Backups: if our database provider keeps automatic backups, deleted data disappears from them as those backups expire.
  • On your device: until you sign out or uninstall the app (or clear the site's data in your browser).

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • have it corrected (rectification);
  • have it deleted (erasure);
  • restrict its processing;
  • receive it in a structured, machine-readable format and have it transmitted to someone else (portability);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl), or the authority where you live or work.

The quickest way to delete everything is in the app: Account → Delete account (see how to delete your account and data). For anything else, email czernekmichal@gmail.com — from your account's address, or tell us which it is, so we can check the data is yours. We answer within one month.

9. Cookies and local storage

Globik sets no cookies and uses no tracking technologies. The web app keeps your progress, settings, language and sign-in session in your browser's local storage, and its files in the browser's cache (a service worker), so it works offline. This storage is strictly necessary for the app you asked to use, stays in your browser and isn't used to track you. You can remove it by clearing the site's data in your browser.

10. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). The database enforces row-level security: each user can read and change only their own progress and settings. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. The app holds only a public key that can't bypass these rules. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach puts your data at risk, we'll tell you and the authority as the law requires.

11. Children

Globik is meant for a general audience who want to learn geography and isn't directed at children under 13. It has no ads, no chat and no way to share content with other users, and an account is optional. If you are under 16, please create an account only with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us an email address, write to us and we'll delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll publish the new version on this page and change the effective date above. If we ever want to use your data, including Google user data, in a new way, we'll tell you in the app first and, where required, ask for your consent.
