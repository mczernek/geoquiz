Learn the world by heart. Globik helps you learn the flag, the capital and the place on the map of every country – all 197 of them – on a colourful 3D globe you can spin and zoom.

PICK WHAT TO LEARN
• Flags, Capitals or Map – one, two or all three at once
• Any mix of continents: Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, South America and Oceania

SIX KINDS OF QUESTIONS
• Pick the right flag out of four
• Name the country a flag belongs to
• Type the capital of a country
• Name the country of a capital
• Name the country highlighted on the globe
• Find a country and tap it on the globe

LEARNS WITH YOU
Globik uses spaced repetition, like flashcards: a question you miss comes back after a few others, and countries you know come back for review after a day, then after four days, then after two weeks. It always knows what to ask next, and you watch your progress grow country by country.

EVERY ANSWER TEACHES
After each answer the globe flies to the country and shows its name, flag and capital – and, if you got it wrong, the country you picked too, so you see where both are.

FORGIVING ANSWERS
Typed answers forgive small typos, missing accents and other spellings, in English or Polish. If Globik still says you're wrong when you were right, tap "I was right" and it counts.

EXPLORE
Tap any country on the globe, or search for a country or a capital, to see its flag, its capital and how well you know it.

AND ALSO
• Streaks, gentle haptics and a little celebration for every country you learn
• A progress screen with every country of your continents
• Works fully offline – no internet needed to learn
• Optional free account to keep your progress and continue on your other devices and on the web
• In English and Polish

Free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking.
